Writing self-review

  • Did you address every part of the prompt?
  • Is your purpose clear and appropriate for the audience?
  • Are grammar, vocabulary, organization, and tone appropriate?
  • Did you finish within the time limit?

Speaking self-review

  • Was your response clear and reasonably fluent?
  • Did you answer directly and develop your idea?
  • Did you add a reason, detail, or example when appropriate?
  • For Listen and Repeat, did you preserve wording and word order?
